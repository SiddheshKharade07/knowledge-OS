KnowledgeOS Test Document — Company FAQ

Q: Where should employees report technical problems?
A: Employees should use the approved IT support channel.

Q: Can employees work remotely?
A: Remote work may be available depending on the role and team requirements.

Q: How should confidential information be handled?
A: Confidential organizational information must only be shared with authorized people.

Q: Who approves planned leave?
A: Planned leave requests are reviewed and approved by the employee's manager.
