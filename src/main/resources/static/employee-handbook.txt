KnowledgeOS Test Document — Employee Handbook

1. Working Hours
Employees normally work from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Teams may have different schedules when approved by their manager.

2. Leave Policy
Employees should submit planned leave requests through the organization's approved leave process. Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving requests.

3. Remote Work
Remote work may be permitted when the employee's role and team requirements allow it. Employees should follow their team's remote-work procedure.

4. Information Security
Employees must protect organizational information and must not share confidential documents with unauthorized people.

5. IT Support
Technical issues should be reported through the organization's approved IT support channel.
