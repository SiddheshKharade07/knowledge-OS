## 1. Purpose & Philosophy

TechGlobal Solutions Inc. ("Company") believes that continuous performance management drives individual growth, team success, and business results. Our approach emphasizes regular feedback, clear expectations, development-focused conversations, and fair, transparent evaluation.

**Core Principles:**

- Growth Mindset: Performance = Current Output + Trajectory + Potential
- Feedback-Rich Culture: Continuous > Annual; Specific > Generic; Forward-looking
- Equity: Calibrated ratings, bias-aware processes, pay-for-performance integrity
- Alignment: Individual Goals → Team OKRs → Department → Company Strategy
- Development First: Every employee has a personalized growth path

### 2.2 New Hires

- 30-Day Check-In: Onboarding progress, early feedback, goal refinement
- 90-Day Review: Formal assessment (probationary review), confirm/extend
- First Full Cycle: Participate in next annual cycle (pro-rated if < 6 months)

### 2.3 Role Changes (Promotion, Transfer, Reorg)

- New goals within 30 days of effective date
- Prior goals: Closed with assessment; achievements carried forward
- Mid-cycle promotion: Off-cycle compensation review eligible

## 3. Goal Setting: OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)

### 3.1 OKR Framework

- Objectives: Qualitative, ambitious, time-bound (quarterly/annual)
- Key Results: Quantitative, measurable, outcome-based (not activity-based)
- Count: 3-5 Objectives; 3-5 KRs per Objective
- Alignment: Cascade (Company → Dept → Team → Individual) + Align (cross-functional)

### 3.2 OKR Quality Standards

- Good KR: "Increase API uptime from 99.9% to 99.99% by Q3" (specific, measurable)
- Poor KR: "Improve system reliability" (vague, not measurable)
- Stretch: 70% achievement = success; 100% = exceptional; < 40% = miscalibrated

### 3.3 OKR Tools & Process

- Platform: Workday (goals) + Ally.io (OKR tracking, visibility)
- Drafting: Employee drafts → Manager review → Collaborative finalization
- Visibility: Public by default (transparency); private option for sensitive
- Tracking: Weekly confidence updates; monthly progress reviews

## 4. Continuous Feedback & Check-Ins

### 4.1 Feedback Mechanisms

- 1:1s: Weekly (30 min minimum); agenda: priorities, blockers, development, wellbeing
- Real-time: CultureAmp (recognize, ask, share) - integrated with Slack/Teams
- Project-based: Retrospectives, peer feedback at milestones
- 360° Feedback: Annual for Managers+ (optional for ICs); anonymous, developmental

### 4.2 Feedback Best Practices (SBI Model)

- Situation: Specific time/context ("In yesterday's client call...")
- Behavior: Observable action ("...you interrupted the client twice...")
- Impact: Effect on others/outcomes ("...which made them feel unheard and cut the demo short")
- Forward: Suggestion/Question ("Next time, try pausing 2 seconds before responding...")

### 4.3 Documentation

- 1:1 Notes: Shared doc (Manager + Employee); key decisions, commitments, follow-ups
- Feedback: CultureAmp (searchable, exportable for reviews)
- No "shadow files" — all performance-related docs in official systems

## 5. Performance Review Process

### 5.1 Review Components

1. Self-Reflection: Achievements, challenges, growth, self-rating (optional)
2. Manager Assessment: Goal achievement, competencies, behaviors, rating
3. Peer/Stakeholder Input: 3-5 nominated reviewers (weighted 20%)
4. Calibration: Cross-team normalization (see Section 6)
5. Final Rating: Calibrated rating → Compensation impact

### 5.2 Rating Scale (5-Point)

| Rating | Label | Description | % Guide | Comp Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Exceptional | Transformational impact; role model; exceeds all goals + creates new value | Top 5% | Merit + LTI |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | Consistently exceeds goals; significant scope/impact; develops others | Next 15% | Merit + LTI |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | Solid achievement of goals; demonstrates core competencies; reliable | Middle 60% | Merit |
| 2 | Developing | Partially meets goals; gaps in competencies; needs support/improvement | Next 15% | Minimal/PIP |
| 1 | Does Not Meet | Significant gaps; unmet goals; behavioral concerns; immediate action needed | Bottom 5% | None/Exit |

### 5.3 Competency Framework (All Roles)

- Technical/Functional Excellence (role-specific)
- Collaboration & Communication
- Problem Solving & Decision Making
- Customer Obsession
- Ownership & Accountability
- Inclusion & Belonging
- Learning Agility
- (Managers+: People Leadership, Strategic Thinking, Business Acumen)

### 5.4 Written Narrative Requirements

- Required for all ratings
- Evidence-based: Specific examples tied to goals/competencies
- Balanced: Strengths + Development areas
- Actionable: Clear next steps for growth

## 6. Calibration & Equity

### 6.1 Calibration Process

- Level 1: Team Calibration (Manager + Skip-Level) — align within team
- Level 2: Department Calibration (Director + VPs + HRBP) — align across teams
- Level 3: Company Calibration (CPO + EVPs + HR) — align across functions, monitor distributions
- Timing: Post-manager draft, pre-finalization (Feb)

### 6.2 Bias Mitigation

- Required: Unconscious Bias Training (all reviewers, annual)
- Tools: Structured rubrics, blind peer feedback option, distribution analytics
- Monitoring: Rating distributions by gender, ethnicity, age, tenure, location
- Audit: Annual equity analysis (internal + external validation)

### 6.3 Calibration Guardrails

- Forced Distribution: NOT used (target distributions are guides, not quotas)
- Minimum Quotas: PROHIBITED
- Rating Inflation/Deflation: Addressed via calibration, not mandates

## 7. Performance Improvement

### 7.1 Early Intervention (Before Formal PIP)

- Trigger: Consistent feedback gaps, missed milestones, competency concerns
- Action: Documented coaching plan (30 days), increased 1:1 frequency, resources
- Goal: Course correction without formal PIP

### 7.2 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

- Trigger: Rating 2 (Developing) with no improvement, or Rating 1, or serious behavioral/performance gap
- Duration: 60 days (standard), 90 days (complex roles), extendable 30 days max
- Structure:
  - Specific, measurable objectives (3-5)
  - Weekly check-ins (Manager + HRBP)
  - Resources: Training, mentoring, tools, workload adjustment
  - Clear success criteria (what "meets" looks like)
- Outcomes:
  - Success: Returns to normal cycle; rating adjusted at next review
  - Partial: Extension (30 days) or role reassignment
  - Failure: Reassignment, demotion, or separation (HR + Legal review)

### 7.3 Documentation & Fairness

- All PIPs: HRBP + Legal review before delivery
- Employee rights: Respond in writing, request mediator, appeal to skip-level
- No surprises: PIP never first conversation about performance gaps

## 8. Career Development & Growth

### 8.1 Career Framework (Dual Track)

| Individual Contributor (IC) Track | Management Track |
| --- | --- |
| IC1: Associate Engineer/Analyst | M1: Team Lead (2-5 reports) |
| IC2: Engineer/Analyst | M2: Manager (5-15 reports) |
| IC3: Senior Engineer/Analyst | M3: Senior Manager (15-50) |
| IC4: Staff Engineer/Analyst | M4: Director (50-200) |
| IC5: Principal Engineer/Analyst | M5: VP (200+) |
| IC6: Distinguished Engineer | M6: SVP / C-Level |

- Equivalent levels: IC3≈M1, IC4≈M2, IC5≈M3, IC6≈M4
- Movement: Bidirectional (IC ↔ Management) with development plan

### 8.2 Promotion Criteria

| Factor | Weight | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sustained Performance | 40% | 2+ cycles at next-level expectations |
| Scope & Impact | 30% | Business results, complexity, influence |
| Competencies | 20% | Demonstrated next-level behaviors |
| Readiness | 10% | Development plan completion, feedback |

### 8.3 Promotion Process

- Nomination: Manager (any time) or Self (with manager support)
- Packet: Promo doc (scope, impact, competencies, peer feedback)
- Committee: VP + Director + HRBP + Peer (cross-functional)
- Decisions: Quarterly (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct) — off-cycle for exceptional cases
- Effective: First day of following month

### 8.4 Individual Development Plans (IDPs)

- Required: All employees (annual, updated mid-year)
- Components: Strengths, Development Areas, 70/20/10 Actions, Timeline, Success Metrics
- 70% Experience: Stretch projects, rotations, shadowing, teaching
- 20% Exposure: Mentoring, coaching, networking, communities
- 10% Education: Courses, certifications, conferences, reading
- Ownership: Employee drives; Manager supports; HR enables

## 9. Learning & Development Programs

### 9.1 Core Programs

- New Hire Orientation: Week 1 (culture, tools, security, compliance)
- Manager Fundamentals: 6-week cohort (new managers, required within 90 days)
- Leadership Development: Emerging Leaders (IC3+), Senior Leaders (M3+), Executive
- Technical Excellence: Architecture, Security, Data, AI/ML, Cloud (certification paths)
- Inclusion: Allyship, Inclusive Hiring, Bias Mitigation, ERG Leadership

### 9.2 Tuition & Certification Support

- Tuition Reimbursement: $10,000/yr (degree relevant to role, B+ grade, 2-yr clawback)
- Certifications: Fully funded (exam + 1 retake + prep materials) for role-relevant
- Conference Budget: $2,000/yr (travel + registration) — see Expense Policy
- Learning Platform: Unlimited access (Pluralsight, Coursera, A Cloud Guru, O'Reilly)

### 9.3 Mentorship & Coaching

- Formal Mentorship: 6-month matches (Mentor: IC4+/M2+; Mentee: IC2+/M1+)
- Flash Mentoring: 1-off sessions (platform-matched)
- Executive Coaching: M3+ (annual budget), IC5+ (case-by-case)
- Peer Coaching: Triads/quads (structured, quarterly rotations)

### 9.4 Internal Mobility

- Internal Job Board: 2-week exclusive posting before external
- Eligibility: 12 months in role, "Meets Expectations"+, Manager awareness
- Process: Apply → Recruiter screen → Hiring Manager interview → Decision (2 weeks)
- Rotation Program: 6-month cross-functional (application-based, competitive)
- Returnship: 16-week program for caregivers returning to workforce

## 10. Compensation Linkage

### 10.1 Merit Increase Guidelines (Annual, Effective Apr 1)

| Rating | Merit Range (Base) | LTI Eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 8-12% | Yes (enhanced) |
| 4 | 5-8% | Yes |
| 3 | 3-5% | Standard |
| 2 | 0-2% | No |
| 1 | 0% | No |

- Ranges are guidelines; final: Manager recommendation + Finance + HR approval
- Market adjustments: Separate from merit (budget: 1-2% of payroll)

### 10.2 Long-Term Incentives (LTI)

- Eligibility: M1+ / IC3+ (Director+ / Staff+)
- Vehicle: RSUs (Restricted Stock Units) — 4-year vest (25% annually)
- Refresh: Annual (performance + tenure + market)
- Performance Share Units (PSUs): VP+ (3-year performance period)

### 10.3 Pay Transparency

- Salary bands: Published internally (per level, geo, function)
- Job postings: Include range (compliance: CA, CO, NY, WA, etc.)
- Employee access: Own band + market data (Workday)
- Annual equity review: Statistical analysis + remediation

## 11. Manager Enablement & Accountability

### 11.1 Manager Expectations

- Conduct weekly 1:1s (no cancellations without reschedule)
- Provide timely, specific, actionable feedback
- Write quality reviews (evidence-based, developmental)
- Support IDPs (approve time/budget, create opportunities)
- Calibrate fairly (participate actively, challenge bias)

### 11.2 Manager Development

- New Manager: 6-week bootcamp + 90-day coaching
- Ongoing: Quarterly Manager Forums, Peer Learning Groups
- Assessment: Upward feedback (360° annual), Team Engagement (eNPS quarterly)
- Accountability: Manager effectiveness in own performance review (20% weight)

### 11.3 HR Business Partner Support

- Partner: Each org (50-200 employees per HRBP)
- Services: Review calibration, PIP guidance, compensation, development, ER
- Escalation: Senior HRBP → VP People → CPO

## 12. Special Situations

### 12.1 Leaves of Absence

- Parental/Medical/Family: Goals paused; cycle extended; no penalty
- Sabbatical: Pre-plan goals; mid-sabbatical check-in optional
- Military: USERRA protections; goals adjusted for service period

### 12.2 Part-Time / Reduced Schedule

- Goals: Pro-rated scope (not expectations of quality)
- Rating: Based on pro-rated goals; same competency standards
- Promotion: Eligible (sustained performance at next level, pro-rated timeline)

### 12.3 Remote / Global Employees

- Same process, same standards
- Time zones: 1:1s during overlap hours; async feedback tools
- Calibration: Cross-geo representation in calibration sessions

## 13. Dispute Resolution

### 13.1 Informal Resolution

- Employee discusses with Manager (documented)
- If unresolved: HRBP facilitation (mediation)

### 13.2 Formal Appeal

- Grounds: Procedural error, bias/discrimination, new evidence, rating inconsistency
- Timeline: 10 business days from rating delivery
- Panel: Skip-Level + HRBP + Peer Manager (not from same team)
- Decision: Final (within 15 business days); documented in Workday
