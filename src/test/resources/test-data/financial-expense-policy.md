## 1. Purpose & Scope

This policy governs all business expenses incurred by TechGlobal Solutions Inc. ("Company") employees, contractors, and authorized representatives. It establishes standards for reasonable, necessary, and properly documented expenditures that qualify for reimbursement under the Company's accountable plan.

**Applies to:** All regular full-time, part-time, and temporary employees.

**Contractors:** Governed by MSA/SOW; expenses billable per contract terms only.

## 2. General Principles

### 2.1 Business Purpose Required

Every expense must have a clear business purpose that benefits the Company. "Business purpose" = Directly related to active conduct of Company business, necessary for role performance, or required for customer/partner engagement.

### 2.2 Reasonableness Standard

Expenses must be reasonable in amount and appropriate for the circumstances. Benchmark: What a prudent person would spend if personally bearing the cost. Luxury upgrades (first-class, luxury hotels, fine dining) require VP+ pre-approval.

### 2.3 Accountable Plan Requirements (IRS)

To maintain tax-free reimbursement status, all expenses must:

- Have business connection
- Be substantiated within 60 days (receipt + business purpose + attendees)
- Excess advances returned within 120 days

Non-compliant expenses become taxable wages.

### 2.4 No Personal Benefit

Expenses primarily for personal benefit are not reimbursable. Mixed-purpose: Only business portion reimbursable (allocation required).

## 3. Pre-Approval Requirements

### 3.1 Mandatory Pre-Approval (Before Incurring Expense)

| Expense Type | Threshold | Approver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| International travel | Any amount | VP + Finance |
| Domestic airfare (non-coach) | Any upgrade | Director + Finance |
| Customer entertainment > $500 | Per event | Director |
| Team events > $1,000 | Per event | Director |
| Capital equipment > $2,500 | Per item | VP + Finance |
| Software/SaaS subscriptions | Any amount | IT + Finance |
| Consulting/professional services | > $10,000 | VP + Legal + Fin |
| Gifts to external parties | > $100 | Director + Legal |
| Political contributions | Any amount | General Counsel |

### 3.2 Pre-Approval Process

- Submit via Concur Request (or ServiceNow for non-travel)
- Include: Business justification, estimated cost, alternatives considered
- Approval valid for 90 days (travel) or 180 days (other)
- Emergency: Verbal VP approval + written within 24 hours

## 4. Travel Expenses

### 4.1 Airfare

- Default: Economy/Coach class for flights < 6 hours
- Premium Economy: Flights 6-8 hours (auto-approved)
- Business Class: Flights > 8 hours OR medical accommodation (HR approved)
- First Class: Never reimbursed (personal upgrade at own cost)
- Booking: Concur Travel (preferred) or direct airline; 14+ day advance purchase
- Changes/Cancellations: Use credits; change fees reimbursed if business-required
- Baggage: 1 checked bag domestic, 2 international (overweight: personal)

### 4.2 Ground Transportation

- Local: Rideshare (Uber/Lyft Business), taxi, public transit
- Rental Car: Compact/Standard; insurance via Company Amex (decline rental co. insurance)
- Parking: Business-related only; airport parking (economy/long-term preferred)
- Mileage: IRS standard rate ($0.67/mile 2024) for personal vehicle business use
- Commute: Home↔Office never reimbursable (including temporary assignments)

### 4.3 Lodging

- Standard: Business-class hotels (Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, IHG preferred)
- Rate caps (per night, before tax/fees):

| City Tier | Cap | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 (NYC, SF) | $350 | NYC, San Francisco, London |
| Tier 2 (Major) | $275 | Chicago, Boston, Seattle, Paris |
| Tier 3 (Secondary) | $200 | Austin, Denver, Atlanta, Berlin |
| Tier 4 (Other) | $150 | All other locations |

- Exceptions: Conference hotel (contracted rate), safety/availability, VP approval
- Incidentals: Room service, minibar, movies — NOT reimbursable

### 4.4 Meals & Incidentals (M&IE)

- Per Diem Method (Preferred): GSA rates (domestic) / State Dept rates (intl)
  - No receipts required for per diem
  - 75% first/last day of travel
  - Deduct meals provided by conference/hotel
- Actual Expense Method: Receipts required for every meal
  - Breakfast: $25 | Lunch: $35 | Dinner: $65 (US tiers; intl adjusted)
  - Alcohol: NOT reimbursable (except approved customer entertainment)
  - Gratuity: Included in limits (max 20%)

### 4.5 Customer/Partner Entertainment

- Business purpose required: Active discussion of specific business matters
- Attendees documented: Names, titles, companies, business relationship
- Limits: $150/person (meals), $300/person (events) without VP approval
- Alcohol: Permitted with meals (moderate); itemized receipt required
- Spouses/partners: Only if business purpose + pre-approval
- Tickets/Events: Face value only; no scalper/resale premiums

## 5. Non-Travel Expenses

### 5.1 Home Office (Remote/Hybrid Employees)

- One-time setup: $500 (monitor, keyboard, mouse, chair, desk accessories)
- Monthly stipend: $50 (internet, electricity, supplies)
- Ergonomic equipment: Up to $1,000 with HR/Ergonomics assessment
- Reimbursement: Via Concur Expense (category: Home Office)

### 5.2 Professional Development

- Annual budget: $2,000 per employee (pro-rated for part-time)
- Eligible: Courses, certifications, conferences, books, subscriptions
- Ineligible: Degree programs (see Tuition Reimbursement), travel to conferences (separate)
- Pre-approval: Manager for > $500; Director for > $1,500
- Reimbursement: Upon completion (certificates) or receipt (books/subscriptions)

### 5.3 Tuition Reimbursement

- Eligibility: 12+ months service; degree relevant to current/future role
- Limit: $10,000/calendar year (undergrad/grad)
- Grade requirement: B or better (Pass = B equivalent)
- Clawback: 2-year service commitment; pro-rated repayment if voluntary departure
- Process: Pre-approval (Manager + HR + Finance) → Reimbursement after grade posted

### 5.4 Technology & Equipment

- All hardware: Ordered via IT ServiceNow (standard catalog)
- Personal purchases: NOT reimbursed (security/asset management)
- Accessories: Up to $200/year for cables, adapters, webcams, headsets
- Phone: Company-provided (standard) or BYOD stipend $50/month

### 5.5 Subscriptions & Memberships

- Professional associations: 1 primary + 1 secondary per year (pre-approval)
- Technical subscriptions: AWS/Azure/GCP certifications, Pluralsight, A Cloud Guru
- News/media: Business-relevant only (WSJ, Economist, TechCrunch+)
- Personal: Netflix, Spotify, gym apps — NOT reimbursable

## 6. Corporate Card Program

### 6.1 Eligibility & Issuance

- Eligible: Director+ and frequent travelers (Manager+ with VP approval)
- Card type: American Express Corporate (Company liability)
- Credit limit: Based on role/travel frequency ($5K-$50K)
- Activation: Required within 30 days; annual re-certification

### 6.2 Cardholder Responsibilities

- Business expenses ONLY; no personal charges (even if reimbursed later)
- Receipts: Upload to Concur within 5 business days of transaction
- Monthly reconciliation: Due by 5th business day of following month
- Lost/stolen: Report immediately (Amex 1-800 + Concur + Finance)

### 6.3 Prohibited Card Charges

- Personal expenses of any kind
- Cash advances
- Alcohol (unless approved entertainment with receipt)
- Gambling, adult entertainment, political contributions
- Fines/penalties (traffic, late fees)
- Gifts > $100 without pre-approval

### 6.4 Violations & Consequences

- 1st offense: Written warning + mandatory training
- 2nd offense: Card suspension (90 days) + personal repayment
- 3rd offense: Card revocation + disciplinary action up to termination
- Personal charges: Immediate repayment via payroll deduction

## 7. Receipt & Documentation Requirements

### 7.1 Receipt Thresholds

- Under $25: No receipt required (per diem or business purpose memo)
- $25 - $75: Receipt or credit card statement + business purpose memo
- Over $75: Itemized receipt REQUIRED (IRS requirement)
- All lodging: Itemized hotel folio required (regardless of amount)

### 7.2 Required Documentation (Per Expense)

- Date, amount, vendor, location
- Business purpose (specific: "Client meeting - Q3 renewal discussion with Acme Corp")
- Attendees (for meals/entertainment): Names, titles, companies
- Project/Client/Cost Center code (for allocation)

### 7.3 Digital Receipts

- Concur mobile app: Photo capture + auto-extract (preferred)
- Email receipts: Forward to receipts@concur.techglobal.com
- E-receipts: Auto-matched for participating vendors (Amex, major hotels, airlines)

### 7.4 Missing Receipts

- Missing Receipt Affidavit (Concur) allowed ≤ 2 times/year per employee
- > $75: Affidavit + manager approval + Finance review
- Pattern of missing receipts: Card suspension + audit

## 8. Submission & Reimbursement Timelines

### 8.1 Submission Deadlines

- Expense reports: Within 30 days of expense date (60 days max for IRS accountable plan)
- Credit card transactions: Auto-import daily; reconcile within 5 business days
- Fiscal year-end (Dec 31): All expenses submitted by Jan 10
- Late submissions: > 60 days = taxable wages; > 90 days = denied (except legal holds)

### 8.2 Approval Workflow

- Employee submits → Manager approves → Finance audit (risk-based) → Payment
- Manager: Validates business purpose, policy compliance, coding accuracy
- Finance: Samples 20% + 100% of > $1,000 items + all exceptions
- Payment: Next payroll cycle (if submitted by Tuesday 12 PM) or following

### 8.3 Reimbursement Methods

- Direct deposit (primary): To payroll bank account
- Corporate card: Company pays Amex directly; employee liable for personal charges
- International: Wire transfer (allow 5-7 business days)

## 9. International Considerations

### 9.1 Currency & Exchange Rates

- Concur uses daily OANDA rates (prev business day)
- Receipts in local currency; Concur converts automatically
- Credit card charges: Use statement conversion rate

### 9.2 VAT/GST Recovery

- Itemized receipts with VAT registration number required
- Finance submits quarterly VAT reclaims (EU, UK, Canada, Australia, etc.)
- Employee: Ensure vendors provide proper VAT invoices

### 9.3 Local Compliance

- Per diems: May differ from US rates (local law mandates)
- Receipt requirements: Some countries require fiscal receipts (Italy, Brazil, India)
- Approval chains: May require local Finance Director sign-off
- Bribery/FCPA: Strict adherence; no facilitation payments; Legal pre-clearance for government officials

## 10. Special Categories

### 10.1 Relocation Expenses

- Policy: Separate Relocation Policy (Global Mobility)
- Covered: Household goods, travel, temporary housing, home sale/purchase assistance
- Tax: Gross-up for taxable benefits (standard)
- Clawback: 2-year service agreement (pro-rated)

### 10.2 Recruiting Expenses

- Candidate travel: Pre-approved by Talent Acquisition + Hiring Manager
- Relocation: Per offer letter + Global Mobility policy
- Agency fees: Pre-approved MSA only; no contingency fees without VP approval

### 10.3 Office Supplies & Expenses

- Central procurement: Office managers order via approved vendors (Staples, Amazon Business)
- Personal purchases: Not reimbursed (use central process)
- Snacks/coffee: Office manager budget; not individual expense

### 10.4 Charitable Contributions

- Company matching: $1:$1 up to $1,000/year/employee (501c3 only)
- Volunteer grants: $250 per 20 hours volunteered (approved orgs)
- Political: NOT matched; NOT reimbursable

## 11. Audit & Compliance

### 11.1 Audit Program

- Continuous: Automated rules (duplicate detection, policy limits, MCC codes)
- Monthly: Finance team reviews 100% of Director+ expenses
- Quarterly: Internal Audit samples 10% of all reports
- Annual: External audit (SOX 404) + T&E specific testing

### 11.2 Common Audit Findings

- Missing itemized receipts > $75
- Personal expenses on corporate card
- Alcohol without approved business purpose
- Upgraded travel without pre-approval
- Splitting expenses to avoid thresholds
- Missing attendee lists for meals/entertainment
- Expenses outside approval authority

### 11.3 Consequences of Non-Compliance

- Expense denial: Non-compliant items removed; employee responsible
- Tax gross-up: Non-accountable plan expenses = taxable wages + gross-up
- Disciplinary: Verbal → Written → Suspension → Termination (progressive)
- Criminal: Intentional fraud = prosecution referral

## 12. Exceptions & Escalation

### 12.1 Exception Requests

- Submit via ServiceNow: "Finance Policy Exception"
- Required: Business case, policy section, alternatives, risk mitigation
- Approval: Controller (≤ $5K), CFO (> $5K), CEO (> $25K)
- Documentation retained 7 years

### 12.2 Escalation Path

- Level 1: Manager (operational questions)
- Level 2: Finance Business Partner (policy interpretation)
- Level 3: Controller (exceptions, disputes)
- Level 4: CFO (policy changes, material exceptions)

## 14. Definitions

- **Accountable Plan:** IRS-compliant reimbursement arrangement (tax-free to employee)
- **GSA Rates:** General Services Administration per diem rates (US domestic)
- **M&IE:** Meals & Incidental Expenses
- **MCC:** Merchant Category Code (credit card transaction classification)
- **Per Diem:** Daily allowance for lodging/meals/incidentals (no receipts required)
- **SBOM:** Software Bill of Materials
- **VAT/GST:** Value Added Tax / Goods and Services Tax
