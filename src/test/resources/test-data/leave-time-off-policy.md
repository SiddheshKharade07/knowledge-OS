## 1. Purpose & Scope

This Leave & Time Off Policy consolidates all Company time-off programs at TechGlobal Solutions Inc. ("Company"). It establishes eligibility, accrual, usage, and administration standards for paid and unpaid leaves, statutory entitlements, and return-to-work processes.

**Applies to:**

- All regular full-time and part-time employees globally
- Contractors/temporary workers: Per MSA/agency terms (not covered by this policy)

**Guiding principles:**

- Health & Wellbeing First: Employees are encouraged to fully use time off
- Compliance: Meets or exceeds all statutory leave requirements
- Consistency: Uniform administration across regions, with local overlays
- Transparency: Clear accrual, balances, and approval expectations

## 2. Governance & Administration

### 2.1 Roles

- People Operations: Policy ownership, program administration
- HR Business Partners: Case management, escalation, manager coaching
- Payroll: Leave pay, accrual, and reconciliation
- Benefits: Insurance/benefit continuation during leave
- Managers: Approvals, coverage planning, return-to-work support
- Employees: Timely requests, documentation, accurate records

### 2.2 Systems

- Time Off Requests & Balances: Workday
- Leave Case Management: Workday Absence Management + ServiceNow (HR)
- Absence Analytics: People Analytics dashboard (monthly)

### 2.3 Regulatory Overlays

- US: FMLA, CFRA, ADA, USERRA, state paid family/medical leave (CA, NY, WA, MA, etc.)
- EU/UK: Statutory annual leave, parental/maternity/paternity, sick pay schemes
- APAC/LATAM: Local statutory minimums (maternity, annual, public holidays)
- Where local law is more generous, local law prevails

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) — Unified Bank

### 3.1 Accrual (Full-Time, US)

| Years of Service | Annual Days | Accrual/Pay Period | Max Carryover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-2 years | 15 days | 4.62 hours | 20 days |
| 3-5 years | 20 days | 6.15 hours | 25 days |
| 6-10 years | 23 days | 7.08 hours | 30 days |
| 11+ years | 25 days | 7.69 hours | 30 days |

- Accrual frequency: Semi-monthly (24 pay periods/year)
- Part-time: Prorated based on scheduled hours
- New hires: Available Day 1 (prorated for partial first year)
- Rehires: Prior service credited if rehired within 12 months

### 3.2 Carryover & Balance Caps

- Maximum carryover: 20-30 days based on tenure (see table)
- Excess above cap: Forfeited Dec 31 unless state law prohibits forfeiture
- Negative balances: Not permitted except payroll error (corrected next cycle)

### 3.3 Usage & Requests

- Submit via Workday at least 5 business days in advance for absences ≥ 3 consecutive days
- Same-day requests permitted for emergencies (manager discretion)
- Minimum increment: 1 hour
- Manager response: Within 2 business days
- Team coverage: Manager may reasonably reschedule for business-critical periods

### 3.4 Separation & Payout

- Accrued, unused PTO paid at separation where required by state law
- Forfeiture states: Balances forfeited unless required by law
- Termination for cause: Payout per applicable state law
- Rehire within 12 months: Restored balance (if not previously paid out)

### 3.5 Blackout & Peak Periods

- Recommended blackout: Nov 15 - Jan 15 (year-end), fiscal close (last week of quarter)
- Critical functions: Pre-approved coverage plans required
- No blanket denial: Approvals balanced against business needs

## 4. Company Holidays & Floating Days

### 4.1 US Holiday Schedule (11 days)

- New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving + Day After, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day

### 4.2 Floating Holidays

- 1 Birthday PTO (use within birth month)

- Regular full-time/part-time (prorated)
- Must work scheduled day before and after (unless approved PTO)

## 5. Sick & Safe Time

- Included in the unified PTO bank (no separate accrual)
- Covered uses: Personal illness/injury, family care, medical appointments, mental health, domestic violence/sexual assault (safe time), public health emergency
- No advance notice for same-day illness; notify manager as soon as practicable
- Medical certification: Required for absences > 3 consecutive days
- COVID-19: Up to 10 additional paid days (separate bank, through Dec 31, 2024)
- State sick-leave laws: Separate statutory banks where mandated (e.g., CA, NY, WA, CO)

## 6. Parental Leave

### 6.1 Entitlements

| Leave Type | Duration | Pay | Eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Birthing Parent | 18 weeks | 100% | 6 months service |
| Non-Birthing Parent | 12 weeks | 100% | 6 months service |
| Adoption/Foster | 12 weeks | 100% | 6 months service |

- Birthing parent: 6 weeks disability recovery + 12 weeks bonding
- Must be taken within 12 months of birth/placement
- Runs concurrently with FMLA/state leave where applicable
- Phased return: Option to work 50% for 4 weeks at 100% pay (manager approval)
- Primary/secondary designation no longer used (equal bonding for all parents)

### 6.2 Benefits Continuation

- Health/dental/vision coverage continues on same terms during paid leave
- Company-paid life/disability continues
- 401(k) match continues on paid leave

## 7. Family & Medical Leave (FMLA / CFRA / State)

- Entitlement: 12 weeks job-protected leave per 12-month period (rolling)
- Eligibility: 12 months service + 1,250 hours in prior 12 months
- Qualifying reasons: Own serious health condition, family member care, bonding, military family exigency, qualifying exigency
- Intermittent/Reduced schedule: Available with medical certification
- Concurrent with Company parental/PTO: Runs simultaneously, not in addition
- Certification: Medical certification within 15 days of request
- Reinstatement: Return to same or equivalent position (no loss of seniority/benefits)
- Key employees: Limited exception per FMLA §825.219 (with notice)

## 8. Bereavement Leave

- Immediate family (spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling): 5 paid days
- Extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt/uncle, step-relations): 3 paid days
- Additional 2 days for travel > 500 miles
- Pregnancy loss: 5 days (birthing parent + partner), regardless of gestational age
- Pet loss: 1 day (discretionary, paid)
- Manager approval; additional unpaid days may be requested

## 9. Military Leave (USERRA)

- USERRA job protection + benefits continuation
- Company supplement: Up to 20 days/year of the difference between military and base pay
- Benefits: Continue for up to 24 months (or longer per USERRA)
- Family military leave: Up to 12 weeks (qualifying exigency, FMLA)
- Reservist drills/annual training: Paid time-off arrangements per manager

## 10. Jury Duty & Witness Leave

- Full pay for first 10 days (less any court stipend)
- Unpaid thereafter (PTO may be used to supplement)
- Notify manager immediately upon receipt of summons
- Provide proof of service; exemption requests only for undue hardship
- Witness/victim leave: Per state law (may be paid, unpaid, or PTO)

## 11. Sabbatical Leave

- Eligibility: 7+ years continuous full-time service
- Duration: 4 weeks paid at 50% base OR 2 weeks paid at 100%
- Frequency: Once per 7-year period
- Notice: 6 months advance notice + manager/VP approval
- Return obligation: 1 year post-sabbatical (else repay paid amount, pro-rated)
- Coverage: Team plan + documentation required before departure

## 12. Disability & Medical Leave of Absence

### 12.1 Short-Term Disability (STD)

- 100% base pay for up to 26 weeks (Company-paid)
- Waiting period: 7 days (PTO may cover)
- Certification: Attending physician statement required

### 12.2 Long-Term Disability (LTD)

- 60% base pay to age 65 (taxable; Company-paid premium)
- Begins after STD exhaustion (180 days total)
- Integration: Coordinates with Social Security and state disability

### 12.3 Personal Medical LOA

- Unpaid job-protected leave beyond FMLA (up to 12 additional weeks, case-by-case)
- Benefits continuation per plan (COBRA-equivalent if unpaid > 30 days)

## 13. Other Leaves of Absence

- Educational Leave: Up to 6 months unpaid (manager + HR approval)
- Personal LOA: Up to 8 weeks unpaid (case-by-case, business need)
- Volunteer/Public Service: Up to 30 days unpaid (approved organizations)
- Victims of Crime/Domestic Violence: Per state law (paid/unpaid)
- Immigrant/Visa-Related: Coordinated with Global Mobility + Legal
- Election Officials: Paid time off for service (per state law)

## 14. Global & Regional Provisions

### 14.1 European Union / UK

- Statutory annual leave: Minimum 20 days + public holidays (28 days total UK)
- Maternity: 52 weeks (39 weeks paid per statutory scheme + Company top-up)
- Paternity: 2-4 weeks paid (Company top-up to 100%)
- Sick pay: Statutory + Company complement

### 14.2 APAC / LATAM / Canada

- Annual leave: Local statutory minimums + Company enhancement
- Maternity/Paternity: Per local law + Company top-up
- Public holidays: Local calendars

### 14.3 Cross-Border Remote Work

- Leave administered per employment entity location
- Tax/immigration implications: Global Mobility review required
- See Remote & Hybrid Work Policy + International Remote Work guidelines

## 15. Return to Work & Accommodations

- Reinstatement: Same or equivalent role; no retaliation for protected leave
- Fitness-for-Duty: Certification may be required for medical leaves > 30 days
- Reasonable Accommodation: ADA/ADAAA interactive process for qualifying conditions
- Lactation Support: Break time + private space for 1 year post-birth (PUMP Act)
- Pregnancy Accommodation: Reasonable adjustments (PWFA)
- Transitional/Phased Return: Available for medical recovery (manager + HR)

## 16. Leave Coordination & Stacking

- Concurrent: Company leave runs with statutory leave (no "stacking")
- Sequence: PTO/complementary pay applied per plan design
- Overlap: Multiple leaves coordinated by People Operations (no double payment)
- Integration: STD/LTD coordinate with Company pay (no pay > 100% of base)

## 17. Shared Leave / Donation Program

- Employees may donate up to 5 PTO days/year to a colleague facing a qualified hardship
- Eligibility for recipients: Serious health event, catastrophic event, military deployment
- Administration: HR committee review; anonymous; documented
- Tax/legal: Per applicable law; no cash equivalents

## 18. Remote & Hybrid Employees

- Same leave policies apply regardless of work location
- Timekeeping: Record all leave in Workday (exempt and non-exempt)
- Availability expectations: Communicate planned leave to team; update status tools
- Digital Nomad: Leave while abroad subject to immigration/tax review

## 19. Tracking, Payroll & Benefits

- Accrual posting: Semi-monthly (Workday), visible to employees in real time
- Payroll: PTO/leave pay on regular pay cycle
- Overpayments: Recovered per applicable law + Finance reconciliation
- Reporting: Monthly absence dashboard (People Analytics); quarterly Board summary
- Audit: Annual leave-administration audit (Internal Audit)

## 20. Non-Compliance & Abuse

- Falsifying leave records or medical documentation: Disciplinary action up to termination
- Misuse of safe/medical leave: Investigation + corrective action
- Manager non-compliance (retaliation, denial of protected leave): Up to termination
- Retaliation prohibited: Good-faith exercise of leave rights is protected

## 23. Definitions

- **PTO:** Paid Time Off

- **CFRA:** California Family Rights Act
- **ADA/ADAAA:** Americans with Disabilities Act / Amendments Act

- **LOA:** Leave of Absence
- **Safe Time:** Protected leave for domestic violence/sexual assault/health emergencies
- **PWFA:** Pregnant Workers Fairness Act

- **Integration:** Coordination of Company pay with disability/state benefits
