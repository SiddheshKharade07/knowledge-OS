## How to Use This FAQ

This document compiles the most frequently asked questions across all major company policies. Answers are summarized for quick reference; full policy details are in the source documents (linked in each section). For policy interpretation questions, contact your HR Business Partner or policy-feedback@techglobal.com.

## Section 1: Working Hours, Attendance & Remote Work

### Q1: What are the core working hours?

Core hours are 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday. You may start between 7:00-10:00 AM and end between 3:00-6:00 PM.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §3.1]*

### Q2: Do I need to track my time?

Non-exempt (hourly) employees: Yes, all hours worked via Workday Time Tracking, due end of each workday. Exempt (salaried) employees: Only PTO, holidays, and exception time.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §3.2]*

### Q3: Can I work remotely?

Eligible after 90 days if your role is designated "Remote-Eligible" by your department VP, performance ≥ "Meets Expectations," no active PIP. Models: Hybrid (3 days on-site Tue/Wed/Thu), Remote-First (≤4 office visits/quarter), Digital Nomad (up to 30 days/year internationally).

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §6]*

### Q4: What equipment does the company provide for remote work?

Laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, docking station. Plus $500 one-time setup stipend and $50/month for internet/utilities.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §6.4]*

### Q5: Can I work from another country temporarily?

Yes, up to 30 consecutive days per country per year with pre-approval (Legal, Tax, Security, People Ops). Must be on the approved countries list (updated quarterly by Global Mobility).

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §6.5]*

## Section 2: Time Off & Leave

### Q6: How much PTO do I get?

Accrual based on years of service (full-time US): 0-2 yrs: 15 days/yr | 3-5 yrs: 20 days | 6-10 yrs: 23 days | 11+ yrs: 25 days. Prorated for part-time. Available Day 1. Max carryover: 20-30 days by tenure.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §5.1]*

### Q7: How do I request time off?

Submit in Workday ≥5 business days in advance for ≥3 consecutive days. Manager approval required. Blackout periods may apply (Nov 15-Jan 15, fiscal year-end).

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §5.1]*

### Q8: What if I'm sick?

Use PTO (no separate sick bank). No advance notice needed for same-day illness. Doctor's note required for >3 consecutive days. COVID-19: up to 10 additional paid days (separate bank, through 2024).

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §5.2]*

### Q9: What parental leave is offered?

Birthing parent: 18 weeks at 100% (6 disability + 12 bonding). Non-birthing parent: 12 weeks at 100% bonding. Adoption/foster: 12 weeks at 100%. Eligibility: 6 months service. Phased return option (50% for 4 weeks at 100% pay).

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §5.3]*

### Q10: What's the bereavement leave policy?

Immediate family (spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling): 5 paid days. Extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt/uncle): 3 paid days. +2 days for travel >500 miles. Pregnancy loss: 5 days (birthing parent + partner).

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §5.5]*

### Q11: Are there company holidays?

11 paid holidays in US (local equivalent globally). Plus 1 floating birthday holiday (use within birth month) and 2 Volunteer Time Off days (8 hrs each) per year.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §4.5]*

## Section 3: Compensation & Benefits

### Q12: When are salary reviews?

Annual merit cycle effective April 1. Reviews completed by March 15. Promotion increases: 8-15%. Market adjustments as needed.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §4.1]*

### Q13: What's the bonus structure?

Individual Contributors: Target 10-20% of base. Managers/Directors: 20-35% + RSUs. VP+: 40-60% + RSUs/PSUs. Sales: Base + commission + accelerators.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §4.2]*

### Q14: What health plans are available?

Medical: HMO, PPO, HDHP/HSA (Company pays 85% employee, 75% dependents). Dental: PPO & DHMO (100% employee, 50% dependents). Vision: VSP (100%/50%). Life/AD&D: 2x base salary company-paid + voluntary up to 5x. Disability: STD 100% (26 wks), LTD 60% (to 65).

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §4.3]*

### Q15: What's the 401(k) match?

100% match on first 6% of eligible compensation (immediate vesting). Roth 401(k) available. Mega Backdoor Roth: after-tax up to IRS limit ($69K total 2024).

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §4.4]*

### Q16: Is there tuition reimbursement?

Up to $10,000/year for degree relevant to role (B+ grade, 2-year clawback). Professional development budget: $2,000/year for courses, certs, conferences, books.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §4.6, §12; Financial Expense Policy §5.3]*

### Q17: What's the ESPP?

Employee Stock Purchase Plan: 15% discount with lookback feature, 2 offering periods per year.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §4.4]*

## Section 4: Expenses & Reimbursements

### Q18: Do I need pre-approval for expenses?

Yes for: International travel (any amount), domestic airfare upgrades, customer entertainment >$500, team events >$1,000, equipment >$2,500, software/SaaS, consulting >$10,000, gifts >$100. Submit via Concur Request.

*[Ref: Financial Expense Policy §3.1]*

### Q19: What's the meal per diem when traveling?

Per diem method preferred (GSA rates domestic, State Dept international). No receipts needed. 75% first/last day. Deduct meals provided by conference/hotel. Actual expense method: Breakfast $25, Lunch $35, Dinner $65 (US). Alcohol not reimbursable.

*[Ref: Financial Expense Policy §4.4]*

### Q20: Can I expense alcohol?

Only with approved customer entertainment (moderate, itemized receipt, business purpose documented). Not reimbursable for personal meals or team events without VP approval.

*[Ref: Financial Expense Policy §4.4, §4.5]*

### Q21: What's the home office stipend?

$500 one-time setup + $50/month (internet, electricity, supplies) for remote/hybrid employees. Ergonomic equipment up to $1,000 with HR assessment.

*[Ref: Financial Expense Policy §5.1; Employee Handbook §4.6]*

### Q22: Do I need receipts for everything?

Under $25: No receipt (memo OK). $25-$75: Receipt or CC statement + memo. Over $75: Itemized receipt REQUIRED (IRS). All lodging: Itemized folio required.

*[Ref: Financial Expense Policy §7.1]*

### Q23: How long do I have to submit expenses?

Within 30 days of expense date (60 days max for IRS accountable plan). Fiscal year-end (Dec 31): Submit by Jan 10. Late >60 days = taxable wages.

*[Ref: Financial Expense Policy §8.1]*

## Section 5: IT, Security & Data Privacy

### Q24: Can I use personal cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox) for work files?

No. CONFIDENTIAL/RESTRICTED data must stay in approved tools: SharePoint, OneDrive, Confluence, Jira, Salesforce, Snowflake. No personal email/cloud for Company data.

*[Ref: IT Acceptable Use Policy §4.2; Data Privacy Policy §9.2]*

### Q25: Can I use ChatGPT or other AI tools for work?

No PII, PHI, CONFIDENTIAL, or RESTRICTED data in public AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot Free). Approved: GitHub Copilot Enterprise, Amazon CodeWhisperer Pro, Azure OpenAI (Company tenant). All AI-generated code requires human review before production.

*[Ref: IT Acceptable Use Policy §4.5]*

### Q26: What if I lose my laptop or phone?

Report IMMEDIATELY: IT ServiceNow ticket + security@techglobal.com. Remote wipe initiated within 1 hour. Police report required for stolen devices. Replacement next business day.

*[Ref: IT Acceptable Use Policy §6.3]*

### Q27: Can I install my own software?

Only software from the Approved Software Catalog (ServiceNow). Cloud services require Cloud Security Assessment. Browser extensions need IT Security review.

*[Ref: IT Acceptable Use Policy §3.2]*

### Q28: How do I report a security incident or phishing?

Security incidents: security@techglobal.com or #security-incident Slack. Phishing: Forward to phish@techglobal.com (no punishment for reporting). Lost credentials: Immediate password reset + MFA re-enrollment.

*[Ref: IT Acceptable Use Policy §9; Security IRP §9.1]*

### Q29: What are my data privacy rights?

Access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection (GDPR/CCPA/Global). Submit via privacy@techglobal.com or web form. Response: 30 days (45 CA). Identity verification risk-based.

*[Ref: Data Privacy Policy §4]*

### Q30: How long is my data kept?

Per Records Retention Schedule. Examples: Employee records 7 years post-separation, Customer contracts 7 years post-termination, Marketing leads 3 years from last engagement, Security logs 13 months/7 years. Legal hold overrides.

*[Ref: Data Privacy Policy §11.2]*

## Section 6: Performance, Career & Development

### Q31: How does the performance review cycle work?

Annual cycle Jan 1-Dec 31. Year-end review Jan 1-31. Calibration Feb. Merit effective Apr 1. Goal setting (OKRs) Apr 1-30. Mid-year check-in Jun 1-30. Ongoing feedback throughout.

*[Ref: Performance Management §2.1]*

### Q32: What are OKRs and how do I set them?

Objectives & Key Results: 3-5 qualitative Objectives, each with 3-5 measurable KRs. Set within 30 days of cycle start/role change. Align: Individual→Team→Dept→Company. Track in Workday/Ally.io. Quarterly reviews with manager.

*[Ref: Performance Mgmt §3]*

### Q33: What are the performance ratings?

5=Exceptional (top 5%), 4=Exceeds (15%), 3=Meets (60%), 2=Developing (15%), 1=Does Not Meet (5%). Calibrated across teams. No forced distribution.

*[Ref: Performance Mgmt §5.2, §6]*

### Q34: What if I'm put on a PIP?

PIP = Performance Improvement Plan (60-90 days, weekly check-ins, clear objectives). Not first conversation — early coaching comes first. Outcomes: Success, extension, reassignment, or separation. HRBP + Legal review required.

*[Ref: Performance Mgmt §7]*

### Q35: How do promotions work?

Dual track (IC: IC1-IC6, Management: M1-M6). Criteria: Sustained performance (40%), scope/impact (30%), competencies (20%), readiness (10%). Nominations quarterly. Committee review. Effective 1st of following month.

*[Ref: Performance Mgmt §8.2, §8.3]*

### Q36: What learning budget do I have?

$2,000/year for courses, certifications, conferences, books, subscriptions. Tuition reimbursement up to $10K/year (degree, B+ grade, 2-yr clawback). Certifications fully funded (exam + 1 retake + prep).

*[Ref: Performance Mgmt §9.2]*

### Q37: Can I transfer to another team?

Internal job board: 2-week exclusive posting. Eligibility: 12 months in role, "Meets Expectations"+, manager awareness. Process: Apply → Screen → Interview → Decision (2 wks). Rotation program: 6-month cross-functional (competitive).

*[Ref: Performance Mgmt §9.4]*

## Section 7: Code of Conduct, Ethics & Compliance

### Q38: How do I report a concern anonymously?

Ethics Hotline: 1-800-TECH-ETHICS (1-800-832-4384) or ethics.techglobal.com. 24/7, anonymous, multilingual. No retaliation for good-faith reports.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §8.2, §14.3]*

### Q39: What's the gift policy?

Acceptable: ≤$100, infrequent, business-appropriate. Prohibited: Cash/gift cards, >$100 without VP approval, government officials (stricter rules). Report gifts >$50 via Ethics Portal within 5 days.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §8.4]*

### Q40: Can I do outside work or serve on boards?

Disclose via annual certification (Workday). Prohibited: Competing businesses, using Company IP for personal gain, influencing vendor selection for personal benefit.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §8.3]*

### Q41: What's the insider trading policy?

No trading on material non-public information (MNPI). Quarterly blackout: 2 weeks before earnings - 2 days after. Pre-clearance required for designated employees (directors, officers, specific roles). Tipping = violation.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §8.5]*

### Q42: What if I see a conflict of interest?

Disclose immediately to Manager + HRBP + Legal. Annual certification in Workday. Examples: Family vendor relationships, investments >1% in competitors, outside employment.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §8.3; Vendor Management §12.1]*

## Section 8: Vendors & Procurement

### Q43: How do I request a new vendor?

Submit via ServiceNow Procurement Request. Thresholds: $0-5K (Manager), $5K-25K (Director + 3 quotes), $25K-100K (VP + RFP), $100K-500K (CFO+Legal+RFP), >$500K (CEO+Board). No splitting to avoid thresholds.

*[Ref: Vendor Mgmt §2.1]*

### Q44: What's required for vendor contracts?

All contracts need: DPA (GDPR), SCCs for intl transfers, security clauses, IP ownership, liability caps (2x fees, uncapped for Tier 1 data breaches), termination clauses, insurance requirements, audit rights. No auto-renewal without opt-out.

*[Ref: Vendor Mgmt §5.2, §5.3]*

### Q45: How are vendors assessed for security/privacy?

Tier 1 (access to restricted data/prod systems): Full assessment + annual review. Tier 2 (confidential data/internal systems): Assessment + biennial review. Tier 3 (no data access): Initial only. Continuous monitoring via SecurityScorecard/BitSight.

*[Ref: Vendor Mgmt §3, §8]*

## Section 9: Emergencies & Business Continuity

### Q46: What happens during a major outage or disaster?

Crisis Management Team activates. Tier 0 services (API, Payments, Auth) target 1-2 hr RTO. Multi-region cloud failover (AWS us-east-1 → us-west-2). Status at status.techglobal.com. Communications every 30 min. Remote work proven capability.

*[Ref: BCDR §3.1, §5, §6, §8]*

### Q47: How will I be notified in an emergency?

PagerDuty (SMS, push, call) + Slack #bcdr-alerts + Email. Update status.techglobal.com. Phone tree for CMT. All-hands only for customer-facing impact or major reputational risk.

*[Ref: BCDR §8.1, §8.2]*

### Q48: What if our building is inaccessible?

Remote-first culture — all employees equipped for full remote work. Split teams across geographies. Alternate sites: Co-working agreements in 10 metros. Mobile command kit: Satellite phones, Starlink, generators.

*[Ref: BCDR §6.2]*

## Section 10: Separation & Offboarding

### Q49: What's the resignation notice period?

2 weeks standard; 4 weeks Director+; 8 weeks VP+. Exit interview (confidential, third-party). Final pay next regular payday (includes accrued PTO payout per state law). Benefits: COBRA info provided; equity vesting per grant terms.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §13.1]*

### Q50: What severance is offered in a layoff?

2 weeks/year service (min 4, max 26 weeks) + 3 months COBRA. WARN Act compliance (60 days notice or pay). Release agreement required for severance > statutory minimum.

*[Ref: Employee Handbook §13.2]*
