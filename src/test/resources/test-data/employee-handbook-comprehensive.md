## 1. Welcome & Company Overview

Welcome to TechGlobal Solutions Inc. ("the Company," "TechGlobal," "we," "us," or "our"). Founded in 2005, TechGlobal is a leading provider of enterprise cloud solutions, serving Fortune 500 companies across 40+ countries. With over 12,000 employees worldwide, we are committed to innovation, customer success, and creating an inclusive workplace where every employee can thrive.

**Our Mission:** Empowering enterprises to transform through intelligent cloud technology.

**Our Values:** Customer Obsession, Integrity, Innovation, Inclusion, Excellence.

This handbook provides a general overview of our policies, practices, and benefits. It is not an employment contract. Policies may be modified at the Company's discretion with appropriate notice. Employees are responsible for reading and complying with all policies referenced herein.

## 2. Employment Classifications

### 2.1 Full-Time Regular Employees

- Scheduled to work 40 hours per week (or local equivalent)
- Eligible for all Company benefits programs
- Paid on a salaried (exempt) or hourly (non-exempt) basis

### 2.2 Part-Time Regular Employees

- Scheduled to work 20-39 hours per week
- Eligible for prorated benefits (medical, dental, vision, 401k match)
- Accrue PTO on a prorated basis

### 2.3 Temporary/Contract Workers

- Engaged for specific projects or time periods (typically ≤ 12 months)
- Not eligible for Company benefits programs
- Managed through approved staffing vendors only

### 2.4 Interns

- Enrolled in accredited educational programs
- Duration: 10-12 weeks (summer) or semester-based
- Eligible for stipend, learning programs, and mentorship

### 2.5 Exempt vs. Non-Exempt Classification (US)

- Exempt: Salaried employees meeting FLSA duties test (executive, administrative, professional, computer, outside sales)
- Non-Exempt: Hourly employees eligible for overtime pay at 1.5x regular rate for hours > 40/week
- Classification reviewed annually or upon role change

## 3. Working Hours & Attendance

### 3.1 Standard Business Hours

- Core Hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM local time (Monday-Friday)
- Standard Workweek: 40 hours (5 days × 8 hours)
- Flexible Start: 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM
- Flexible End: 3:00 PM - 6:00 PM

### 3.2 Timekeeping Requirements

- Non-exempt employees: Must record all hours worked via Workday Time Tracking
- Exempt employees: Record PTO, holidays, and exception time only
- Time entries due by end of each workday; corrections within 5 business days
- Managers approve timecards weekly by Monday 10:00 AM

### 3.3 Attendance & Punctuality

- Expected to be available during core hours
- Notify manager ≥ 1 hour before shift for late arrival/absence
- 3+ unexcused absences in 90 days → Performance Improvement Plan
- Excessive tardiness (> 15 min, 3+ times/month) documented

### 3.4 Overtime (Non-Exempt Only)

- Pre-approval required from manager for all overtime
- Overtime paid at 1.5x regular rate (hours > 40/week)
- Double-time for hours > 12/day or > 8 on 7th consecutive day (CA, AK, NV)
- Unauthorized overtime paid but subject to disciplinary action

## 4. Compensation & Benefits

### 4.1 Compensation Philosophy

- Market-competitive: Target 50th-75th percentile of peer group
- Annual merit cycles: Effective April 1 (reviews completed by March 15)
- Promotion increases: 8-15% base salary increase
- Market adjustments: As needed based on compensation surveys

### 4.2 Base Salary & Variable Pay

- Individual Contributors: Base + Annual Bonus (target 10-20% of base)
- Managers/Directors: Base + Annual Bonus (target 20-35%) + LTI (RSUs)
- VP+: Base + Annual Bonus (target 40-60%) + LTI (RSUs/PSUs)
- Sales Roles: Base + Commission (varies by role) + Accelerators

### 4.3 Health & Welfare Benefits (US)

- Medical: 3 plan options (HMO, PPO, HDHP/HSA) — Company pays 85% employee, 75% dependents
- Dental: PPO & DHMO — Company pays 100% employee, 50% dependents
- Vision: VSP network — Company pays 100% employee, 50% dependents
- Life/AD&D: 2x base salary (Company-paid) + voluntary supplemental up to 5x
- Disability: STD (100% base, 26 weeks), LTD (60% base, to age 65)
- HSA: Company contributes $500 individual / $1,000 family annually (HDHP only)
- FSA: Healthcare ($3,200 max) & Dependent Care ($5,000 max)

### 4.4 Retirement & Financial Wellness

- 401(k): 100% match on first 6% of eligible compensation (immediate vesting)
- Roth 401(k) option available
- Mega Backdoor Roth: After-tax contributions up to IRS limit ($69,000 total 2024)
- ESPP: 15% discount, lookback feature, 2 offering periods/year
- Financial Planning: 4 free sessions/year via Ayco/Financial Engines

### 4.5 Time Off Benefits

- See Section 5 for detailed leave policies
- 11 Company-paid holidays (US) / local equivalent globally
- Volunteer Time Off: 2 paid days/year (8 hours each)
- Birthday PTO: 1 floating holiday (use within birth month)

### 4.6 Additional Perks

- Wellness Reimbursement: $600/year (gym, apps, equipment, etc.)
- Home Office Stipend: $500 one-time + $50/month (remote/hybrid)
- Learning Budget: $2,000/year (courses, certifications, conferences, books)
- Commuter Benefits: Pre-tax transit/parking up to IRS limit ($315/month 2024)
- Employee Referral Bonus: $2,000-$10,000 (varies by role level)

## 5. Leave Policies

### 5.1 Paid Time Off (PTO) — Unified Bank

Accrual Rates (Full-Time, US):

| Years of Service | Annual Days | Accrual/Pay Period | Max Carryover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-2 years | 15 days | 4.62 hours | 20 days |
| 3-5 years | 20 days | 6.15 hours | 25 days |
| 6-10 years | 23 days | 7.08 hours | 30 days |
| 11+ years | 25 days | 7.69 hours | 30 days |

- Prorated for part-time employees
- New hires: Available on Day 1 (pro-rated for partial first year)
- Minimum increment: 1 hour
- Request ≥ 5 business days in advance for ≥ 3 consecutive days
- Manager approval required; blackout periods may apply (Nov 15-Jan 15, fiscal year-end)

### 5.2 Sick & Safe Time

- Included in PTO bank (no separate sick bank)
- Use for: Personal illness, family care, medical appointments, domestic violence
- No advance notice required for same-day illness
- Doctor's note required for > 3 consecutive days
- COVID-19: Up to 10 days additional paid leave (separate bank, through 2024)

### 5.3 Parental Leave

- Birthing Parent: 18 weeks paid at 100% (6 weeks disability + 12 weeks bonding)
- Non-Birthing Parent: 12 weeks paid at 100% (bonding)
- Adoption/Foster: 12 weeks paid at 100% (effective placement date)
- Eligibility: 6 months continuous service
- Job protection: FMLA + state equivalents; return to same/equivalent role
- Phased Return: Option to work 50% for 4 weeks at 100% pay (manager approval)

### 5.4 Family & Medical Leave (FMLA/CFRA/State)

- 12 weeks job-protected leave (rolling 12-month period)
- Eligibility: 12 months service + 1,250 hours worked
- Qualifying reasons: Serious health condition, family care, military exigency
- Runs concurrently with Parental Leave where applicable
- Intermittent/Reduced schedule leave available with medical certification

### 5.5 Bereavement Leave

- Immediate Family (spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling): 5 paid days
- Extended Family (grandparent, in-law, aunt/uncle): 3 paid days
- Additional 2 days for travel > 500 miles
- Pregnancy loss: 5 days (birthing parent + partner)

### 5.6 Military Leave

- USERRA compliance + Company supplement
- Up to 20 days paid military leave/year (difference between military & base pay)
- Benefits continuation for up to 24 months
- Job protection per federal/state law

### 5.7 Jury Duty & Witness Leave

- Full pay for first 10 days (less court stipend)
- Unpaid thereafter; PTO may be used
- Notify manager immediately upon summons

### 5.8 Sabbatical Leave

- Eligibility: 7+ years continuous full-time service
- Duration: 4 weeks paid at 50% base (or 2 weeks at 100%)
- Once per 7-year period
- Requires 6-month advance notice + manager/VP approval
- Must return for 1 year post-sabbatical or repay

## 6. Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

### 6.1 Eligibility

- All regular full-time/part-time employees after 90 days
- Role must be designated "Remote-Eligible" by department VP
- Performance rating ≥ "Meets Expectations" in last review
- No active PIP or disciplinary action

### 6.2 Work Models

- On-Site: 5 days/week at designated office (required for lab, hardware, security roles)
- Hybrid: 3 days on-site (Tue/Wed/Thu core) + 2 days remote
- Remote-First: ≤ 4 office visits/quarter (designated roles, VP approval)
- Digital Nomad: Up to 30 days/year working from approved international locations

### 6.3 Remote Work Requirements

- Dedicated workspace meeting ergonomic & security standards
- Reliable high-speed internet (≥ 25 Mbps down / 10 Mbps up)
- Available during core hours (10 AM - 3 PM ET/PT based on team)
- Webcam-on for all scheduled meetings
- Annual home office safety self-assessment

### 6.4 Equipment & Support

- Company provides: Laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, docking station
- $500 one-time setup + $50/month stipend (internet, utilities)
- IT support: Remote diagnostics + advance replacement hardware
- VPN required for all corporate resource access

### 6.5 International Remote Work

- Pre-approval required: Legal, Tax, Security, People Ops sign-off
- Maximum 30 consecutive days per country per year
- Approved countries list maintained by Global Mobility (updated quarterly)
- Employee responsible for visa/tax compliance; Company provides guidance only

## 7. Performance Management

### 7.1 Performance Cycle

- Annual cycle: January 1 - December 31
- Mid-year check-in: June 1-30
- Year-end review: January 1-31 (following year)
- Ratings: Exceeds (5), Strongly Meets (4), Meets (3), Partially Meets (2), Does Not Meet (1)

### 7.2 Goal Setting (OKRs)

- Objectives & Key Results set within 30 days of cycle start/role change
- 3-5 Objectives per cycle; 3-5 Key Results per Objective
- Alignment: Individual → Team → Department → Company
- Quarterly OKR reviews with manager

### 7.3 Feedback & Development

- Continuous feedback encouraged (CultureAmp platform)
- 360° feedback for managers+ (annual, anonymous)
- Individual Development Plan (IDP) required for all employees
- Career conversations: Minimum quarterly

### 7.4 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

- Trigger: "Partially Meets" or "Does Not Meet" rating, or serious performance gap
- Duration: 60-90 days with weekly check-ins
- Clear, measurable objectives with defined success criteria
- Outcomes: Successful completion, extension, reassignment, or separation

## 8. Code of Conduct & Ethics

### 8.1 Core Principles

- Act with integrity: Honest, transparent, accountable
- Respect others: Inclusive, professional, zero tolerance for harassment
- Protect Company assets: Information, IP, physical, financial
- Comply with laws: All applicable regulations globally
- Speak up: Report concerns without fear of retaliation

### 8.2 Anti-Harassment & Discrimination

- Protected classes: Race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, genetic information, and any other protected by law
- Harassment includes: Unwelcome conduct creating hostile environment
- Sexual harassment: Quid pro quo + hostile environment
- Reporting: Manager, HR Business Partner, Ethics Hotline (anonymous, 24/7)
- Investigation: Prompt, fair, confidential to extent possible
- Consequences: Up to and including termination

### 8.3 Conflicts of Interest

- Disclose: Outside employment, board seats, investments > 1% in competitors/vendors, family relationships with vendors
- Annual certification required (Workday)
- Prohibited: Competing businesses, using Company IP for personal gain, influencing vendor selection for personal benefit

### 8.4 Gifts & Entertainment

- Acceptable: ≤ $100 value, infrequent, business-appropriate
- Prohibited: Cash/gift cards, > $100 without VP approval, government officials (stricter rules)
- Report: All gifts > $50 via Ethics Portal within 5 business days

### 8.5 Insider Trading

- Prohibited: Trading on material non-public information (MNPI)
- Blackout periods: Quarterly (2 weeks before earnings - 2 days after)
- Pre-clearance required: All trades in Company stock (directors, officers, designated employees)
- Tipping: Sharing MNPI with others who may trade = violation

### 8.6 Anti-Corruption & Bribery (FCPA/UK Bribery Act)

- Zero tolerance for bribery of government officials or commercial parties
- Facilitation payments prohibited
- Third-party due diligence required for all agents/intermediaries
- Accurate books & records mandatory

## 9. Information Security & Data Privacy

### 9.1 Information Classification

- PUBLIC: Marketing materials, public filings
- INTERNAL: Employee directory, internal memos, non-sensitive docs
- CONFIDENTIAL: Customer data, financials, roadmaps, contracts
- RESTRICTED: PII, credentials, encryption keys, M&A info, trade secrets

### 9.2 Data Handling Requirements

- CONFIDENTIAL/RESTRICTED: Encrypt at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Need-to-know access only; quarterly access reviews
- No personal email/cloud storage for Company data
- Approved tools only: SharePoint, OneDrive, Confluence, Jira, Salesforce, Snowflake
- Data retention: Per Records Retention Schedule (Legal owns)

### 9.3 Privacy (GDPR/CCPA/Global)

- Data Subject Rights: Access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection
- Privacy by Design: DPIA required for new systems processing personal data
- Breach Notification: 72 hours (GDPR), without unreasonable delay (CCPA)
- Vendor DPAs required for all processors
- Contact: privacy@techglobal.com

### 9.4 Security Incident Reporting

- Report immediately: security@techglobal.com or #security-incident Slack
- Lost/stolen devices: IT ServiceNow ticket + remote wipe within 1 hour
- Phishing: Forward to phish@techglobal.com (no punishment for reporting)
- Annual security training mandatory (90% completion = bonus metric)

### 9.5 Acceptable Use

- Company systems for business purposes; incidental personal use permitted
- No: Illegal content, harassment, crypto mining, unauthorized software, bypassing controls
- Monitoring: Company reserves right to monitor all systems (subject to local law)
- BYOD: MDM enrollment required for corporate email/data access

## 10. Workplace Safety & Health

- Report hazards: safety@techglobal.com or Safety Portal

- Alcohol: Permitted at approved Company events only

## 13. Separation & Offboarding

### 13.1 Voluntary Resignation

- Notice: 2 weeks standard; 4 weeks for Director+; 8 weeks for VP+
- Exit interview: Confidential, third-party administered
- Final pay: Next regular payday (includes accrued PTO payout per state law)
- Benefits: COBRA information provided; equity vesting per grant terms

### 13.2 Involuntary Separation

- Performance: Documented PIP completion required (except gross misconduct)
- Reduction in Force: WARN Act compliance (60 days notice or pay)
- Severance: 2 weeks/year service (min 4, max 26 weeks) + 3 months COBRA
- Release agreement required for severance > statutory minimum

### 13.3 Offboarding Process

- Day 1: Notification, transition plan, knowledge transfer
- Week 1: Access review, equipment return, account closures
- Day 15: Final access revocation, alumni network invitation
- References: HR-only verification (dates, title); no performance commentary
